Turtle book: Pi checks before printing
======================================

Checked on the Pi on 2026-09-04 (ucblogo 6.2.2-3+b1, python3-pcbasic
2.0.5-1; the book was developed against 6.2.2-2 and 2.0.4-3, and nothing
below turned on the difference).


Results
-------

[x] Sound in PC-BASIC works. The Music section of chapter 19 and the
    BEEP in COUNTDOWN.BAS stay.

[x] Ctrl+C in BASIC breaks a running program, as the books say.

[x] Stopping Logo: the Logo menu's Stop item works. Alt+S does not
    respond on the Pi (possibly no Alt shortcut does). The book now
    says so and presents Stop as a mouse action only.

[!] save / load in Logo did NOT round-trip. Cause found: the menu was
    starting Logo and BASIC from the home directory, so saved files
    (mine.lg, STARS.BAS) landed loose in /home/girls. The menu now
    starts Logo in kidbox/logo and BASIC in kidbox/basic, and the
    starter files moved to a root-owned kidbox/presets. Re-test after
    reinstalling:

        save "mine.lg   then   bye, reopen Logo,   load "mine.lg   then   pots

    If load still brings nothing back while the file exists in
    kidbox/logo, look at the file itself (cat ~girls/kidbox/logo/mine.lg):
    save should have written the "to ... end" blocks.


Still to glance at (does not change the text)
---------------------------------------------

[ ] Screen fit. The figures were drawn in an 820x430 Logo window. On the
    Pi's screen everything will be at least that big, but  street  (three
    houses) and  spiral 10  are the widest drawings; look at them once.


Already verified without the Pi (for the record)
------------------------------------------------

- All 31 programs run without error (tools/check-programs.sh), including
  the keyboard game, which was driven with simulated keys.
- FILL fills, SETLABELHEIGHT enlarges labels, and Logo menu -> Stop
  interrupts a runaway recursion in the wx UCBLogo window.
- PC-BASIC: a FOR/NEXT busy loop takes no measurable time, so the
  countdown uses PLAY "P2" (a one-second rest) instead. Measured at one
  second in both the headless and the graphical interface, with and
  without an audio device.
- The four Logo error messages quoted in chapter 13 are UCBLogo's actual
  wording.
